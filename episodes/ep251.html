SIMPLIFIED SPEECH #251

Popular Baby Names

Andrew and Indiana compare common and unusual names, changing baby-name trends, naming traditions in North America and Korea, and the role of family names, middle names, religion, culture, and identity.

Duration31 min
LevelB1–B2
TopicNames, family and culture
Language assets12

EPISODE OVERVIEW

What is this episode about?

The hosts explore why people choose certain names and why parents today increasingly value individuality. They share the stories behind their own names, discuss how traditions differ across cultures, and explain practical features such as nicknames, family names, and middle names.

本期内容概要

Andrew 和 Indiana 从自己的名字讲起，讨论父母为什么选择常见或独特的名字，以及当代父母为何越来越重视个性。他们还比较了北美与韩国的取名传统，并谈到宗教、家族传承、中间名、昵称和文化身份如何影响一个人的名字。

KEY QUESTION

Would you choose a traditional or unique name for a child?

Answer once before reading and once after finishing.

PART 01

Common names and unique names

ENGLISH GUIDE

Andrew introduces the topic of popular and unusual baby names. His own name is extremely common, while Indiana’s name is much more distinctive, so the two hosts are at opposite ends of the spectrum. Their contrast opens a wider conversation about naming culture in English-speaking countries.

中文对照

Andrew 介绍本期主题：流行与特别的婴儿名字。他自己的名字非常常见，而 Indiana 的名字更有辨识度，因此两人可谓处在光谱的两端。这样的对比也引出了英语国家取名文化的讨论。

PART 02

The story behind Indiana’s name

ENGLISH GUIDE

People often assume Indiana was named after the US state or Indiana Jones. In fact, her mother—an artist inspired by Indigenous art—wanted her first daughter’s name to carry meaning and symbolism. Indiana has sometimes grown tired of jokes and references connected with her name.

中文对照

人们常以为 Indiana 的名字来自美国印第安纳州或电影《夺宝奇兵》。其实，她身为艺术家的母亲深受原住民艺术启发，希望长女的名字带有意义和象征。这个独特名字也让 Indiana 有时厌倦相关玩笑和典故。

PART 03

Andrew, James, and family connections

ENGLISH GUIDE

Andrew’s first name has a biblical origin and was very common in his generation. His middle name, James, connects him with relatives on both sides of his family. Amusingly, an older cousin was also named Andrew only a few months before him, making Andrew the family “copycat.”

中文对照

Andrew 这个名字源于《圣经》，在他那一代十分常见。他的中间名 James 则把他与父母两边的亲人联系起来。有趣的是，一位只比他大几个月的表哥也叫 Andrew，所以他笑称自己是家里的“模仿者”。

PART 04

Why popular names are becoming less dominant

ENGLISH GUIDE

Baby-name data show a major shift toward variety. In 1950, top-ten names accounted for a much larger share of newborns than they do today. The hosts connect this change with modern individualism: many parents want a child’s name to feel personal and to stand out rather than simply fit the norm.

中文对照

婴儿姓名数据体现出明显的多样化趋势。1950 年，前十名姓名在新生儿中的占比远高于今天。两位主持人认为，这与现代社会重视个性有关：许多父母希望孩子的名字更私人化、更突出，而不是只求符合常规。

PART 05

Creativity, celebrities, and practical limits

ENGLISH GUIDE

Celebrity culture and social media may encourage parents to choose eye-catching names or unusual spellings. A distinctive name can be memorable, but the hosts also note a practical downside: a child may spend a lifetime correcting other people’s spelling and pronunciation.

中文对照

名人文化和社交媒体可能促使父母选择抢眼的名字或特别拼法。独特的名字容易被记住，但也有现实代价：孩子可能一生都要不断纠正别人对自己名字的拼写与发音。

PART 06

Korean naming traditions

ENGLISH GUIDE

In Korea, the family name comes first and is followed by the given name. Some families traditionally share one syllable among members of the same generation. Korea also has far fewer surnames than the United States, so names such as Kim, Lee, and Park are shared by very large numbers of people.

中文对照

在韩国，姓在前、名在后。有些家族传统上会让同一辈成员共享名字中的一个音节。韩国的姓氏数量也远少于美国，因此 Kim、Lee、Park 等姓氏拥有非常庞大的人群。

PART 07

Middle names and the names we go by

ENGLISH GUIDE

Middle names may honour relatives, sound good with a first name, or provide an alternative when several people share the same first name. Andrew’s brother goes by his middle name, John, because his first name is the same as their father’s. Indiana also used her middle name at university as a fresh start.

中文对照

中间名可以纪念亲人、与名字搭配得更悦耳，也可以在多人同名时提供替代称呼。Andrew 的弟弟因为与父亲同名，所以平时使用中间名 John。Indiana 上大学时也曾改用自己的中间名，把它当作一次全新的开始。

PART 08

Names carry culture and identity

ENGLISH GUIDE

Canada’s multicultural population illustrates how families carry naming traditions across borders: some people have no middle name, while others have several. The hosts close by inviting listeners to share popular names and naming customs from their own countries and cultures.

中文对照

加拿大的多元文化人口说明，家庭会把取名传统带到新的国家：有些人没有中间名，有些人却有好几个。节目最后，两位主持人邀请听众分享自己国家和文化中的热门名字及取名习俗。
