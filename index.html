CULIPS · CATCH WORD #294

Touch Grass

如何用英语谈论“泡在网上”“与现实脱节”，以及这些表达在真实交流中的语气差异。

EPISODE SUMMARY

本期内容摘要

English

This episode introduces two modern internet expressions: touch grass and chronically/terminally online. The hosts explain that “touch grass” is a dismissive way to tell someone to log off, reconnect with reality, and spend time with real people. “Chronically online” and “terminally online” describe people who spend too much time online, with “terminally” sounding more exaggerated and humorous.

中文

本期介绍了两个现代网络表达：touch grass 以及 chronically/terminally online。节目说明，touch grass 常用于让某人停止沉迷网络、重新接触现实生活和真实的人；而 chronically online 与 terminally online 都用于形容一个人长期泡在网上，其中 terminally 的夸张和调侃意味更强。

KEY EXPRESSION 01

touch grass

语气较强

To tell someone that they spend too much time online and need to reconnect with real life.

自然中文

少上点网，回归现实吧；出去接触一下真实世界；别再沉迷网络了。

核心语感

它并不是字面上的“去摸草”，而是一种带有批评、否定或嘲讽意味的网络表达。通常用于一个人过度沉迷网络争论、明星八卦、阴谋论或某个小众网络圈子，以至于对现实产生扭曲认识的情况。

使用提醒

直接对别人说 Go touch grass 往往显得不耐烦、轻蔑或冒犯。它更常见于网络争论、熟人之间的玩笑，或第三人称吐槽。

Podcast example
John’s a nice guy, but he really needs to touch grass and stop believing in these crazy ideas.

John 人不错，但他真的应该少泡在网上，别再相信这些疯狂的想法了。

Natural example
You’ve been arguing on Reddit for six hours. Go touch grass.

你已经在 Reddit 上吵了六个小时了。出去走走、回归现实吧。

KEY EXPRESSIONS 02–03

chronically online vs. terminally online

chronically online

含义：长期花很多时间上网，已经形成持续性的习惯。

语气：轻度批评，也常用于自嘲。

I’m chronically online. I check my phone the moment I wake up.

我真是长期泡在网上，一醒来就看手机。

terminally online

含义：严重沉迷网络，夸张地形容“上网成瘾到没救”。

语气：比 chronically online 更强、更戏谑。

Everyone’s so terminally online these days.

现在大家都像是严重沉迷网络一样。

表达重点常见语气
chronically online长期上网过多轻微批评／自嘲
terminally online夸张强调沉迷戏谑／夸张
touch grass已经需要回归现实强烈／轻蔑／不耐烦

ACTIVE OUTPUT

把输入变成输出

Speaking question

Do you think people are becoming too chronically online? Why or why not?

I think many people are becoming chronically online because our phones are always within reach. Even when we are not actively scrolling, notifications constantly pull our attention back online. I sometimes catch myself doomscrolling before bed, so I’m trying to replace that habit with reading. I don’t think everyone needs to “touch grass,” but most of us could benefit from spending more time away from screens.
